# How do I reset my password?

**Placeholder FAQ, not real Swiss Life content.**

## Self-service reset
1. Go to the self-service password portal and click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your employee ID or corporate email address.
3. Confirm with your registered MFA method (authenticator app or SMS).
4. Choose a new password: at least 12 characters, with upper- and lower-case letters, a digit, and a symbol. You cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.

## Account locked
After 5 failed login attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. If you are still locked out after that, call the service desk and they will unlock it once they have verified who you are.

## Lost MFA device
If you lost your MFA device, you cannot use self-service. Open a ticket in the category **Access & Identity > MFA reset**. You will need to verify your identity by video call.

VPN / remote access FAQ (placeholder, not real Swiss Life content)

How do I connect to the VPN?
Open the VPN client, choose your region gateway, and sign in with your corporate account plus MFA. On first use your device must be enrolled in device management.

The VPN connects but internal sites do not load.
Disconnect, flush DNS (ipconfig /flushdns on Windows), and reconnect. If it still fails, open a P3 ticket in category Network > VPN and attach the client log.

Can I use the VPN on a personal device?
No. Only managed corporate devices may connect. For webmail and Teams, use the browser-based access instead.

# How do I open a ticket?

**Placeholder FAQ, not real Swiss Life content.**

## Channels
- **Service portal** (preferred): New request > choose a category > describe the issue.
- **Email**: send to the service desk mailbox. A ticket is created automatically.
- **Phone**: for urgent (P1/P2) issues only.

## What to include
- A short summary, e.g. "Outlook crashes when opening calendar"
- When it started, and whether other people are affected
- Error messages or screenshots
- Your location and device asset tag

## Priorities and response times
| Priority | Example | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Business-critical system down for many users | 15 min |
| P2 | Major function degraded, no workaround | 1 h |
| P3 | Single user affected, workaround exists | 8 business hours |
| P4 | Request or question | 2 business days |

You can track the status of your ticket in the service portal under **My tickets**.
